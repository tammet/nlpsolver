The original passage and question are shown first. The logic translation may
use different words or structures for the same idea. Propose a small number of
implication rules that connect those representations.

For isa, the order is ["isa", CLASS, THING]. Variables beginning with ? are
universally quantified. The same variable in two atoms denotes the same thing.

PREMISE means the current clauses may supply the atom. CONSEQUENCE means a
clause has a place where the atom could be useful. BOTH means either position
is possible. These are mechanical hints, not claims that the atom is already
provable.

A rule may express ordinary background knowledge or a connection stated by
this passage. Preserve who does what to whom. Do not add an extra premise when
that premise is exactly the information the rule is supposed to provide.

Taxonomy example:
RULE: ["isa","bassoon","?X"] -> ["isa","woodwind","?X"]

Event/participant example:
RULE: ["has type","?E","teach"] AND ["has actor","?E","?P"] -> ["isa","teacher","?P"]

The second example classifies the actor ?P, not the event ?E. Argument identity
and argument order matter.

Write at most twelve RULE: lines. Each rule has one to three premises and one
consequence. Use only displayed atoms and displayed nested shapes. Do not solve
