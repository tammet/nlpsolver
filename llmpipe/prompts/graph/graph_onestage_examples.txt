INPUT:

Birds can fly. Penguins are birds that cannot fly. Tweety is a penguin. Can Tweety fly?

OUTPUT:

["and",
 ["@id","S1",["and",
   ["holds","W0",["forall","X",["implies",["isa","bird","X"],["normally",["isa","can_fly","X"]]]]],
   ["@p","S1",0.95]]],
 ["@id","S2",["holds","W0",["forall","X",["implies",["isa","penguin","X"],["isa","bird","X"]]]]],
 ["@id","S3",["holds","W0",["forall","X",["implies",["isa","penguin","X"],["not",["isa","can_fly","X"]]]]]],
 ["@id","S4",["holds","W0",["isa","penguin","Tweety 1"]]],
 ["@id","S5",["question",["isa","can_fly","Tweety 1"]]]]

(Capability mode gives the "can_" prefix; the negation stays outside the name;
the same concept name "can_fly" is used in the rule, the exception and the
question.)

INPUT:

Butte is in Montana. St Pierre is not in Montana. A city can only be in one state. Butte and St Pierre are in the same state?

OUTPUT:

["and",
 ["@id","S1",["holds","W0",["and",["isa","city","Butte 1"],["isa","state","Montana 2"],["in","Butte 1","Montana 2"]]]],
 ["@id","S2",["holds","W0",["and",["isa","city","St Pierre 3"],["not",["in","St Pierre 3","Montana 2"]]]]],
 ["@id","S3",["holds","W0",["forall","X",["forall","Y",["forall","Z",["implies",
     ["and",["isa","city","X"],["isa","state","Y"],["isa","state","Z"],["in","X","Y"],["in","X","Z"]],
     ["=","Y","Z"]]]]]]],
 ["@id","S4",["question",["exists","Y",["and",["isa","state","Y"],["in","Butte 1","Y"],["in","St Pierre 3","Y"]]]]]]

(The relation "in" keeps subject-first order in every unit; "only one" is a
uniqueness rule with an identity conclusion; the negative fact uses ["not",...].)

INPUT:

Someone wanted to improve their health. If they exercise regularly, they will increase their fitness level. If they improve their diet, they will lose weight. They either start exercising or improve their diet. Morgan did not increase their fitness level. Did Morgan lose weight?

OUTPUT:

["and",
 ["@id","S1",["holds","W0",["and",["isa","person","Someone 1"],["isa","wants_to_improve_health","Someone 1"]]]],
 ["@id","S2",["holds","W0",["forall","X",["implies",["and",["isa","person","X"],["isa","exercises_regularly","X"]],["isa","increases_fitness_level","X"]]]]],
 ["@id","S3",["holds","W0",["forall","X",["implies",["and",["isa","person","X"],["isa","improves_diet","X"]],["isa","loses_weight","X"]]]]],
 ["@id","S4",["holds","W0",["forall","X",["implies",["isa","person","X"],["or",["isa","exercises_regularly","X"],["isa","improves_diet","X"]]]]]],
 ["@id","S5",["holds","W0",["and",["isa","person","Morgan 2"],["not",["isa","increases_fitness_level","Morgan 2"]]]]],
 ["@id","S6",["question",["isa","loses_weight","Morgan 2"]]]]

(The conditionals about the discourse subject are rules over persons, so
Morgan 2 falls under them. "start exercising" and "exercise regularly" are the
same construction in this case and take the same name; the "either ... or" is
plain "or"; every generic object is folded.)

INPUT:

OUTPUT:

["and",
 ["@id","S1",["holds","W0",["isa","inherited_characteristic","height"]]],
 ["@id","S2",["holds","W0",["forall","X",["implies",["isa","inherited_characteristic","X"],
     ["isa","passed_from_parent_to_offspring_by_genetics","X"]]]]],
 ["@id","S3",["question",["forall","X",["implies",["isa","offspring","X"],["normally",["can_inherit_from_parents","X","height"]]]]]]]

("height" is an abstract kind the text talks about as a thing, so it is a kind constant. In S2 the parent, offspring and
genetics are generic participants that no unit treats as things in their own

subject is the offspring and "height" is a kind constant the case talks
about, so it stays an argument of a relation with the modality in the name.)

INPUT:

Rocks interacting with moving water over long periods of time cause weathering. Sometimes weathering smooths rocks. A pebble is a kind of small rock. Waves are made of moving water. Is it true that waves interacting with pebbles can smooth pebbles?

OUTPUT:

["and",
 ["@id","S1",["holds","W0",["forall","X",["implies",
     ["and",["isa","rock","X"],["interacts_with","X","moving_water"]],
     ["causes","X","weathering"]]]]],
 ["@id","S2",["and",
   ["holds","W0",["forall","X",["implies",["isa","rock","X"],["normally",["smooths","weathering","X"]]]]],
   ["@p","S2",0.5]]],
 ["@id","S3",["holds","W0",["forall","X",["implies",["isa","pebble","X"],["and",["isa","rock","X"],["isa","small_for_a_rock","X"]]]]]],
 ["@id","S4",["holds","W0",["forall","X",["implies",["isa","wave","X"],["made_of","X","moving_water"]]]]],

     ["normally",["isa","can_smooth_pebbles","X"]]]]]]]

("moving water" and "weathering" are kinds shared
by several units, so they stay arguments. The pebbles of the question are a

same verb "interact" is a relation in S1 because its object there is a shared
kind constant; when both objects are shared kind constants, keep the relation
in both places.)
