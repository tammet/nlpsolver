CRITICAL RULES — VERIFY BEFORE FINALIZING OUTPUT:

1. THREE ITEMS PER ATOM (§2.2): every content atom is [NAME, ARG1, ARG2].
   Concepts use ["isa", CONCEPT_NAME, ENTITY]; everything else is an open
   relation [RELATION_NAME, LEFT, RIGHT]. No other predicate shape exists.

2. NAMES ARE LOWERCASE AND UNDERSCORED (§2.3): no spaces, capitals or
   punctuation; built from the unit's own words; modifiers and complements
   that change the meaning stay in the name; no vague labels.

3. RESERVED NAMES NEVER APPEAR (§2.2): not "is rel2", "has property",
   "has degree property", "have", "has part", "has type", "has actor",
   "has target", or any name with a space. Role names (agent, theme,
   recipient, source, destination, location, instrument, time, manner) only
   on a reified event variable.

4. FOLD OR ARGUMENT, CONSISTENTLY (§2.5): concrete entities and bound
   variables are arguments; generic, indefinite or absent complements fold
   into the name; one shape per verb or construction across the whole case.

5. GRADABLE ADJECTIVES CARRY THE CLASS (§2.4): a word in ASU.adjectives with a
   relclass other than "none"/"entity" becomes "word_for_a_class"
   ("big_for_a_mouse"), never bare "big".

6. NEGATION OUTSIDE, MODALITY INSIDE (§2.6, §2.7): "not" is always
   ["not", ...]; "can_", "must_", "may_", "usually_", "formerly_" stay in the
   name; capability mode gives "can_".

7. REIFY ONLY THREE-OR-MORE PARTICIPANT EVENTS (§2.8): a subject and one
   object is a relation atom; three or more participants use an event
   variable with the fixed roles and an open event type on the variable.

8. ENTITY IDS FROM STAGE 1 (§4.1): use ids exactly as given; never a url;
   the wh placeholder id never appears; scope "kind" generics are constants.

9. GENERIC ENTITIES -> VARIABLES OR FOLDED (§4.2): every other generic entity
   is either a fresh bound variable or folded into a name; never a bare
   constant.

10. WH-QUESTIONS USE "ask" (§7.1); bare-plural yes/no questions use
    forall + normally (§7.2a); explicit universals and class-membership
    questions use strict forall (§7.2b); polarity mirrors the surface (§7.3).

11. THE QUESTION IS NOT A FACT (§7.4): never assert the question's content in
    a "holds" package; keep every modifier of the question (§7.5).

12. NO NESTED IMPLIES (§5.7); strict_rule never "normally"; normal_rule has
    "normally" on the consequent; frequency adverbs go into @p (§5.8).

13. WORLD IS ALWAYS "W0" (§0.2): no next, time, location, mental, definite,
    @time, $setof, $count, $measure, "<", ">", "xor".

14. ONE @id PER ASU (§0.3), exactly one question package in the case, no
    free or reused variables.
