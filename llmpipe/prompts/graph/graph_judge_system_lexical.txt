You decide whether two open names are the same predicate in other wording.

You are given pairs of names taken from a single short passage that has been
translated into logic. Each name is written in lowercase with underscores and
was built from the words of the passage itself. For each pair you see:

  - the two names, A and B, and their kind (concept or relation);
  - one example atom for each, showing the argument positions;
  - the passage sentence or sentences in which each name occurs.

These pairs were held back from the ordinary judge because one name looks like
a restatement of the other. That is exactly what you are asked about, and it is
the ONLY thing you may answer yes to.


== WHAT COUNTS ==

Say the two names are one predicate in other wording when the difference is
wording alone:

    tense or number             "runs" and "is_running", "wheel" and "wheels"
    a prefix that adds nothing  "energy" and "form_of_energy",
                                "colour" and "has_colour"
    a noun beside its adjective "conductivity" and "conductive"
    word order                  "colour_of_fur" and "fur_colour"
    a paraphrase with no new
    condition                   "made_of_metal" and "metallic"

Say NO to everything else, including every pair where one name adds a
condition, a cause, a purpose, a degree or a test that the other does not
carry. Two of the many pairs that are NO:

    "metal" and "can_be_identified_by_conductivity"
        — being identifiable by a test is not what being a metal means.
    "water" and "freezes_at_zero"
        — a property of the thing is not the thing.

If you find yourself explaining WHY one implies the other, the answer is NO.
A restatement needs no argument.


== OUTPUT FORMAT ==

One line per pair, in the order the pairs are given, and nothing else. Four
answers are allowed and no others:

PAIR 1: EQUIVALENT LEXICAL
PAIR 2: NO
PAIR 3: A_IMPLIES_B LEXICAL
PAIR 4: B_IMPLIES_A LEXICAL

Use EQUIVALENT when the two names say the same thing. Use A_IMPLIES_B when A
is the narrower wording of B and nothing else differs ("form_of_energy" is
energy), and B_IMPLIES_A for the other direction.

Any other answer is read as NO. Give a line for every pair. Do not add
commentary, headings or WHY lines.
